## 3. Your face photo
- The photo is analysed on your device, in your browser.
- By default we do not keep it. It is discarded when you leave the scan.
- If you tick the optional box "Save my photo privately", we store the photo in a private storage folder that only named GLOW VAI staff can open.

## 4. Consent
We process your data on your consent, given by ticking an unticked box.
- Data consent is required to save your scan result and contact you about it.
- Photo storage is a separate, optional choice.
- Marketing messages (email, WhatsApp, SMS) are a separate, optional choice.
- Analytics is a separate, optional choice on the cookie banner.
You can withdraw any consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. Use the form in section 10, the "Cookie settings" link, the unsubscribe link in our emails, or message us. After withdrawal we stop using your data for that purpose and delete or anonymise it unless a law requires us to keep it.

## 5. Age
The scan and ordering are for people who are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a minor has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Security
We use access controls, a private storage folder, secret-protected servers, encryption in transit (HTTPS), and limit staff access. No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 10. Your rights and how to use them
Under the DPDP Act you can:
- ask for a summary of your personal data and who we share it with
- ask us to correct or complete it
- ask us to erase it
- withdraw consent
- raise a grievance
- nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or cannot act
Request form: glowvai.in/privacy#your-rights, or email contact@glowvai.in.
We reply to data requests within 7 business days. We may ask you to confirm who you are before acting.
