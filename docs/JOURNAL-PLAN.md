# GLOW VAI Journal Content Strategy & Execution Plan

This document outlines the editorial standards, SEO guidelines, 10 launch articles, publishing schedule, and conversion integration strategy for the GLOW VAI Journal.

---

## 1. Non-Negotiable Editorial Rules

| Rule | Requirement & Compliance Reason |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Original Content Only** | Never copy or closely paraphrase Minimalist, The Derma Co, or third-party blogs. Prevents copyright issues and Google duplicate content penalties. |
| **No Medical Claims** | Use conservative phrasing ("may help", "supports"). Never use "cures", "treats", or "heals". No prescription advice. |
| **Dermatologist Review** | Every published article must be reviewed by a certified dermatologist, displaying "Reviewed by: Dr. TODO" with verifiable credentials. |
| **Sourced Scientific Facts** | Ingredient facts must be conservative and cite authoritative dermatological sources or clinical studies at the end (E-E-A-T compliance). |
| **Honest Catalog Disclosure** | Mention catalogue products only where genuinely relevant, with explicit notice: *"We sell this product. Our picks are not paid placement by the brand."* |
| **Real Author & Schema** | Author credit assigned to GLOW VAI founders. Structured `Article` and `Person` JSON-LD schema generated for each post. |
| **Conversion Funnel End** | Every article ends with a free face scan call-to-action and double opt-in newsletter signup box. |
| **Format Standards** | 900 to 1,400 words per article, single H1 title, clear H2 sections, 40 to 60 word top summary, zero em-dashes, zero banned words. |
